## 2. In-Depth Asset Specifications

---

### Asset 01: `hero-treatment-still-life`
- **Section:** 02. Hero Section (Module A — Personalized Rx Match)
- **Intended UX Purpose:** Establish immediate clinical legitimacy, modern pharmaceutical refinement, and tangible treatment reality without sterile hospital aesthetics.
- **Subject, Setting & Composition:**
  - Modern matte subcutaneous auto-injector pen paired with an amber glass dropper bottle and a single minted scored tablet.
  - Arranged with architectural balance upon a warm, honed travertine limestone slab.
- **Casting & Representation:** Still life (no human figures).
- **Lighting & Mood:** Soft, raking morning sunlight from the upper-left casting gentle, warm directional shadows. Tranquil, tactile, bespoke.
- **Palette & Background:** Warm bone linen background (`#FBF9F5`), amber glass reflections (`#C68B59`), brushed champagne metal hardware, soft cream travertine (`#F2EDE4`).
- **Aspect Ratio & Dimensions:** 4:3 (Desktop: 600 × 450px rendered; Native master: 1200 × 900px).
- **Negative Space & Focal Point:** Focal point anchored in bottom-right two-thirds; clean negative space in upper-left for floating Rx badge overlay.
- **Responsive Cropping:** Centered crop for mobile; preserves horizontal expanse on desktop.
- **Format & Optimization:** WebP, 85% quality, lossy compression, target payload `< 95 KB`.
- **Negative Constraints:** Avoid cheesy stock pharmacy plastic bottles, garish blue hospital backdrops, fake brand logos (e.g. no counterfeit Novo Nordisk or Eli Lilly branding), and cluttered medical equipment.

### Asset 02: `hero-patient-vitality`
- **Section:** 02. Hero Section (Module B — Patient Progress & Vitality)
- **Intended UX Purpose:** Provide human empathy and relatable emotional relief. Shows an authentic adult experiencing quiet, grounded well-being rather than ecstatic, unrealistic celebration.
- **Subject, Setting & Composition:**
  - Natural adult subject (mid-30s to early 40s) in comfortable, high-quality linen clothing, gazing thoughtfully toward natural window light with subtle, peaceful confidence.
  - Setting: Modern, sunlit domestic architectural interior with muted earth tones and clean lines.
- **Casting & Representation:** Diverse, relatable, natural human appearance. No excessive cosmetic makeup; authentic skin texture, subtle freckles, and un-airbrushed lines.
- **Lighting & Mood:** Diffused golden hour / morning daylight. Warm, honest, calming, dignified.
- **Palette & Background:** Warm neutrals, muted terracotta accents (`#A35C3E`), deep espresso tones (`#241C1A`), and soft bone walls.
- **Aspect Ratio & Dimensions:** 4:5 (Desktop: 500 × 625px rendered; Native master: 1000 × 1250px).
- **Negative Space & Focal Point:** Subject framed on the right; negative space on the left allows non-intrusive UI overlay chips (e.g., *"Ongoing provider check-ins"*).
- **Responsive Cropping:** Center-top crop for mobile viewports (retaining face and upper torso).
- **Format & Optimization:** WebP, 82% quality, target payload `< 110 KB`.
- **Negative Constraints:** Strictly avoid fake before-and-after tropes, measuring tapes around waists, sad-to-happy split frames, or exaggerated gym-fitness poses.
