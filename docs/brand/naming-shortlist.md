## 2. Evaluation Matrix for Top Candidates

Candidates were evaluated across 6 core criteria (scored 1 to 5):
1. **Pronunciation & Phonetics:** Instant phonetic ease across English-speaking audiences.
2. **Memorability & Recall:** Stickiness without cognitive confusion.
3. **Emotional Resonance:** High trust, zero clinical anxiety, destigmatizing warmth.
4. **Category Elasticity:** Equally credible for Weight Loss, Hair, Skin, Sexual Health, and Longevity.
5. **Conflict & Confusion Risk:** Distinct from existing category incumbents (Hims, Ro, Hers, Noom, Lemonaid).
6. **Visual & Typographic Potential:** Looks exceptional set in editorial serif or modern grotesque letterforms.
