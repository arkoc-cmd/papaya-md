## 1. Brand Voice, Tone & Lexicon Guidelines

### Voice Pillars
1. **Clinical Rigor without Hospital Chill:** Speak with physician-level precision, but with the warmth, empathy, and tactile grace of an editorial publication. Never sound bureaucratic or sterile.
2. **Destigmatizing & Respectful:** Treat sensitive conditions (metabolic resistance, hair thinning, hormonal decline, intimate health) as biological realities, never personal failings or vanity obsessions.
3. **Radical Economic & Process Clarity:** Eliminate the anxiety of traditional healthcare—no surprise copays, no confusing pharmacy handoffs, no hidden subscription lock-ins.
4. **Physician-Led Integrity:** Emphasize continuous human clinical supervision. We are not an automated pill dispenser or an algorithm-only dispensary.

### Vocabulary Guardrails
| Approved Terms | Prohibited / Discouraged Terms | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Personalized clinical protocol* | *Quick fix, miracle cure, magic shot* | Avoids deceptive pharmaceutical outcomes and maintains medical credibility. |
| *Licensed clinician / board-certified physician* | *Automated approval, instant prescription* | Communicates real medical oversight and state regulatory compliance. |
| *Compounded medication from 503A pharmacies* | *Generic knockoff, off-brand clone* | Medically and legally accurate designation for customized pharmaceutical formulations. |
| *Evidence-based care* | *Guaranteed weight loss, 100% regrowth* | FTC and FDA compliance against unsubstantiated efficacy claims. |
| *Discreet, unbranded doorstep packaging* | *Secret delivery, stealth shipment* | Professional discretion rather than illicit or secretive undertones. |

### Section 09: Evidence-Based Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

```
[Eyebrow] FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Headline: Everything you need to know about Alivio.
```

#### Question 1: Do I need health insurance to use Alivio?
- **Answer:** No. Alivio operates independently of private insurance carriers and Medicare/Medicaid. Our direct-to-consumer model allows us to offer transparent, fixed monthly rates without surprise hospital bills, prior-authorization denials, or high insurance deductibles. You may also use an HSA or FSA card for eligible clinical fees and prescription medications `[Unverified Claim]`.

#### Question 2: How does the doctor determine if I am eligible for treatment?
- **Answer:** After you submit your secure online health intake, a licensed physician or nurse practitioner reviews your symptoms, medical history, current medications, and health markers. If clinically indicated, your provider issues an electronic prescription tailored to your needs. If the provider determines that a requested medication is not safe or medically appropriate for your profile, you will receive clinical recommendations, and you will not be charged for medication fulfillment.

#### Question 3: What are compounded medications, and are they safe?
- **Answer:** Compounding is the practice of preparing personalized medications tailored to the unique clinical needs of an individual patient (for example, combining two complementary active ingredients or tailoring an exact dosage strength). Alivio partners exclusively with licensed U.S. 503A compounding pharmacies that adhere to stringent state board of pharmacy regulations and strict cGMP standards. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved, but their active pharmaceutical ingredients are sourced from FDA-registered facilities `[Unverified Claim]`.

#### Question 4: How does delivery and packaging work?
- **Answer:** Medications are shipped via expedited courier in plain, unmarked exterior packaging. For temperature-sensitive therapies (such as liquid GLP-1 peptide formulations), shipments include insulated liners and cold gel packs to maintain product stability throughout transit. You will receive real-time discreet tracking updates via SMS or email.

#### Question 5: How often will I check in with my clinician?
- **Answer:** Telehealth continuity is a cornerstone of our care model. You will have structured asynchronous clinical check-ins before every dosage titration or refill milestone. Furthermore, you can message your medical team at any time through the secure patient portal with questions regarding dosage, side-effect mitigation, or lifestyle modifications.

#### Question 6: Can I pause or cancel my subscription at any time?
- **Answer:** Yes. You are never locked into a multi-month contract. You can pause upcoming shipments, update your delivery address, or cancel your care subscription at any time directly through your Alivio patient account dashboard prior to your next refill fulfillment date.
