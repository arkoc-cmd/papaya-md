# Project Brief — Consumer Telehealth Brand & Homepage

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Context
- **Project Purpose:** Establish a high-trust, consumer-facing telehealth brand identity and responsive homepage.
- **Brand Status:** New consumer brand; brand name is currently undecided (working placeholder / agnostic design system).
- **Target Audience:** Adult men and women seeking accessible, discreet, evidence-informed healthcare.
- **Platform Infrastructure:** **Alivio Telehealth Infrastructure** serves as the underlying clinical/telehealth platform technology; it is **not** the consumer-facing brand.
- **Creative Reference:** Inspired by **Hims** for typographic confidence, editorial polish, approachable tone, and consumer-health clarity.
  > *Strict Non-Infringement Notice: No copying of Hims (or any reference brand's) brand identity, trade dress, trademarks, verbatim copy, bespoke illustrations, or proprietary assets.*

## 3. Scope of Care & Category Validation Framework
The service mix will cater to both men and women across core modern telehealth categories. The exact category mix and emphasis will be validated during planning:
1. **Weight Management / Metabolic Health** (e.g., GLP-1s, lifestyle protocols — pending clinical verification)
2. **Hair & Scalp Health** (e.g., preventative and regrowth regimens)
3. **Dermatology & Skin Health** (e.g., acne, aging, personalized topical care)
4. **Sexual Wellness & Hormonal Health** (destigmatized solutions for men & women)
5. **Everyday / Longevity & Preventative Health** (routine consultations, preventative biomarkers)

## 4. Epistemic Classification Framework
In adherence to strict clinical integrity, all statements and data across the project must be tagged:
- `[Research Observation]` Direct findings from verified benchmarks and competitive analysis.
- `[Assumption]` Operational or user behavior premises subject to client/stakeholder confirmation.
- `[Hypothesis]` Design, UX, or CRO ideas to be prototyped and tested.
- `[Unverified Claim]` Any prospective health or outcome statement that requires licensed medical or legal sign-off before publication.

---

## 5. Architectural & Technical Directives
- **Markup:** Semantic HTML5 (`header`, `main`, `section`, `nav`, `article`, `footer`, accessible ARIA roles).
- **Styling:** Modern Vanilla CSS (Design Tokens, CSS Custom Properties, Fluid Type using `clamp()`, Flexbox/Grid, Container Queries). No Tailwind CSS.
- **Interactivity:** Modular Vanilla JavaScript (ES modules, clean DOM event delegation, no external runtime dependencies).
- **Animation:** GSAP & ScrollTrigger used sparingly and purposefully, adhering strictly to `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Framework Restrictions:** Zero frontend frameworks (No React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, etc.) and no Python scripts or build tools.
- **Responsive Baseline:** Mobile-first (390px baseline), expanding gracefully to tablet (768px - 834px) and desktop (1280px - 1440px+).
