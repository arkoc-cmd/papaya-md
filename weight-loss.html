What is the difference between Semaglutide and Tirzepatide?
Semaglutide is a single GLP-1 receptor agonist that mimics the GLP-1 hormone, producing an average of ~15% body weight reduction in clinical trials. Tirzepatide is a dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist that targets two distinct metabolic pathways, resulting in up to 20.9% average body weight loss. Your prescribing physician will evaluate which formulation matches your health history and goals best.
What if I am afraid of needles or injections?
PapayaMD offers a 100% needle-free alternative: our daily sublingual dissolving GLP-1 tablet. It dissolves comfortably under your tongue for rapid absorption into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive breakdown of swallowed pills with zero injections required.
Are these brand-name drugs like Ozempic, Wegovy, or Mounjaro?
No. Our medications are compounded formulations prepared exclusively by accredited, state-licensed 503A and 503B compounding pharmacies in the United States. Compounded medications contain the same active pharmaceutical ingredients (Semaglutide or Tirzepatide base) as brand-name drugs, but are customized by pharmacies and are not FDA-approved brand-name products.
