Clinical Overview & Mechanism
Estradiol transdermal patches deliver 17β-estradiol, chemically and biologically identical to endogenous human ovarian estradiol. Continuous transdermal delivery provides consistent physiologic serum levels, bypassing hepatic first-pass metabolism to minimize liver enzymatic load.
Application Routine & Skin Site Rotation
Apply to a clean, dry, lotion-free area of skin on the lower abdomen or upper buttocks. Press firmly for 10 seconds. Do not apply to the breasts or irritated skin. Rotate application sites each change to prevent local skin irritation.
Ingredients & Transdermal Matrix
Active Ingredient: Bioidentical 17β-estradiol USP.
Inactive Components: Medical-grade acrylic adhesive polymer matrix, ethylene-vinyl acetate copolymer backing film, polyester release liner. Latex-free and hypoallergenic.
Safety, Contraindications & Prescribing Notes
Estrogen therapy carries important clinical precautions. Women with an intact uterus should use combined progesterone to protect the endometrium. Contraindicated in individuals with active venous thromboembolism, history of estrogen-dependent neoplasia, or unexplained vaginal bleeding. Discuss all medical history with your PapayaMD clinician.
